## Yoyu v1.8.0

**Five characters instead of one, a disconnect that actually disconnects, and a
battery gauge on the AMOLED board.**

### Pick a character

The kitsune is no longer the only one. `/settings` offers five, and each is a
gauge. Every one of them shows how much headroom is left, in its own way:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitsune** | fans out one to three tails |
| **Moon** | waxes through its phase |
| **Candle** | burns down |
| **Plant** | grows, a leaf pair at a time |
| **Cat** | its ball of yarn gets bigger |

Four of the five are continuous, where the tails are three buckets. A moon at
47% looks like 47% instead of rounding into "middling". That is the reason to
offer a choice at all, so the picker names the gauge next to the name.

The four new ones share a cell grid that feeds both surfaces. The panel paints
it, and the board's own page emits the same thing as SVG, so the glass and the
page cannot drift apart. The kitsune keeps its own renderer; it has sub-cell
mood features nothing else needs.

Characters keep fixed colours across all six themes, as the kitsune always did.
One that changed colour with the theme would stop being the same character.

### Disconnecting is as easy as connecting

Pairing a board takes one click. Disconnecting meant knowing that `/connect`
existed, on a page whose own first line says it is for people who *cannot* run
the companion, and finding a grey button at the bottom of it. The tray had
nothing at all.

There is now a **Claude account** section on `/settings`, behind a
confirmation, saying plainly what goes and what stays. The login and the top-up
key go. Wi-Fi, theme, character, screens and history stay. The tray grows
**Disconnect from Claude** beside **Pair this board**, per board where you run
two. From a terminal, `companion.py --disconnect`.

> **A disconnect that did not disconnect.** Underneath this was a real bug. The
> board persists its top-up key in flash, and disconnecting cleared it only in
> RAM, so the next reboot read it straight back. A "disconnected" board came
> back still accepting top-ups from whatever last paired with it, and kept a
> live credential in flash besides. Fixed. The key is erased, and the board now
> answers the old one with a 401.

Both ends forget together. The board revokes its key and the computer drops its
copy, so no dead key is left to produce a confusing refusal later.

### The AMOLED board has a battery gauge

`boards.h` said "no battery divider is documented for this board", which was the
wrong question. There is no divider because the board does not need one. It
carries an **AXP2101** power-management chip that already measures the cell and
keeps a fuel gauge, and it has been answering at `0x34` on the I²C bus the whole
time.

So the percentage now comes from the gauge itself, not from a voltage curve
fitted by hand the way the 2″ board has to.

**`battery` and `charging` are in `/api/status` now**, for both boards. The
gauge was drawn on the glass and nowhere else, so "is it on battery, and how
flat" could only be answered by walking over and looking at it.

### AMOLED touch: it is the panel

Earlier notes left this unsolved. It is now settled, and the docs said the wrong
thing about it.

Waveshare's own driver reports no touch either, using their `SensorLib`, their
pin definitions, and no Yoyu code. The controller's interrupt line never fires.
Their schematic then rules out the wiring. Display and touch share one 24-pin
FPC at `J40`, the display works through it, and the touch controller answers on
I²C through it. Neither the CST9220 nor the CO5300 appears on the mainboard
schematic at all, so both are bonded to the panel module.

The dead link is between the controller and the sensing grid it sits on, inside
that module. **Do not open the case.** There is nothing in there to reseat. A
board that does this needs replacing.

### Upgrading

Over the air from `/update`, or re-flash from the setup page. Wi-Fi, login,
settings and history all survive.
