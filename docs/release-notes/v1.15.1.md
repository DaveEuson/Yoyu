## Yoyu v1.15.1

**Running out no longer takes over the screen**, and the warning that replaces
it now works on every screen that shows your headroom.

### The Timer takeover is gone

When a window hit zero, the board used to switch itself to the Timer screen and
show a countdown to the reset. It had three problems:

- It **overrode a screen you had deliberately chosen.**
- It **did nothing at all if you had switched Timer off** — which is exactly the
  board with the least else to tell you how long the wait is.
- Since v1.15.0, **the screen you are already on says it.** Meters, Focus and
  Micro draw an exhausted window as what it is: zero, in your theme's alarm
  colour, pulsing.

So the board leaves the screen alone now. Timer is still there if you want the
countdown; it just no longer forces itself on you.

### The pulse now reaches Meters and Focus

v1.15.0 drew Meters and Focus in your theme's layout, but only Micro was ever
redrawn to animate the pulse. On the other two, an exhausted window sat there
in red without moving. All three pulse now.

### A correction to the v1.15.0 notes

The v1.15.0 notes said the Timer takeover had been removed. It had not — it was
still there in that release, working exactly as before, and the pulse only
animated on Micro. Both of those are what this release fixes. The v1.15.0 notes
have been corrected to say what actually shipped.

### Upgrading

Over the air from `/update`, or re-flash from the setup page. Wi-Fi, login,
settings and history all survive.
